## MINIATURE — PROMPT READY TO DEPLOY

**Concept retenu :** Grille Chrono (Francis + chrono 00:20 + cascade de miniatures)

**Complémentarité titre/miniature :**
- Titre = le CONTEXTE : "J'ai créé 10 miniatures en 20 minutes (avec l'IA)"
- Miniature = le VISUEL : Francis + chrono + cascade = la PREUVE visuelle de la promesse

```
# ROLE:
You are a World-Class YouTube Thumbnail Designer & CTR Strategist. 
You specialize in "High-Gloss" Tech aesthetics, mastering the balance 
between vibrant lighting and clean readability.

Resolution: 1280 x 720 pixels

---

# TASK:
Create a dramatic thumbnail showing a confident man looking at camera 
with a slight smirk, holding up his phone showing a stopwatch/timer 
displaying "00:20" (20 minutes). Behind him, a cascade of 10 stunning 
YouTube thumbnails fans out like a deck of cards. The concept: 
"10 thumbnails, 20 minutes — not a joke."

---

# 1. CHARACTER & SKIN PHYSICS (CRITICAL):
* **Reference:** Use the provided photos as the anatomical base.
* **Pose:** Right hand holding up a smartphone toward camera (screen 
  visible showing timer "00:20"). Left hand relaxed. Head slightly 
  tilted, expression: calm confidence with a knowing smirk — "yeah, 
  it's that fast."
* **Skin Texture:** Hyper-realistic but "polished." Healthy skin with 
  detailed pores, avoiding the "plastic AI" look.
* **Natural Tones:** KEEP skin tones natural (beige/tan). DO NOT 
  oversaturate the face with red or heavy orange.
* **Eyes:** Crystal clear, bright iris, white sclera, with sharp 
  reflections (catchlights). Looking straight at camera.

---

# 2. ADVANCED LIGHTING SETUP (THE "POP" FACTOR):
* **Multi-Source Lighting:** NEVER use flat lighting.
* **Key Light:** Soft, bright light hitting the center of the face 
  for perfect visibility.
* **Dual Rim Lights (Crucial):** Purple (#9D4F9E) rim light on the 
  left side. Cyan rim light on the right side. Sharp colored edges 
  along face, neck, and shoulders for powerful 3D separation.
* **Phone screen glow:** The phone screen emits a soft white/green 
  glow that subtly illuminates the character's hand and chin from below.
* **Specular Highlights:** Subtle, shiny highlights on forehead, nose 
  bridge, and cheekbones — premium luminous look (not sweaty).
* **Thumbnail cascade glow:** The fanned-out thumbnails behind emit 
  a soft purple (#9D4F9E) ambient glow.

---

# 3. THE THUMBNAIL CASCADE (KEY VISUAL):
* **What's behind him:** 8-10 YouTube thumbnails fanned out like a 
  deck of cards or a poker hand, spreading from lower-left to 
  upper-right behind the character.
* **Each thumbnail is different** — varied styles, colors, compositions 
  — demonstrating volume and variety.
* **The thumbnails overlap slightly** and are arranged with a natural 
  fan/cascade perspective.
* **Glow effect:** Subtle purple (#9D4F9E) edge glow on each thumbnail.
* **The front-most thumbnail is sharpest**, the ones behind gradually 
  blur, creating depth.

---

# 4. BACKGROUND (STRICT):
* **Deep dark background** — near black with subtle dark navy (#011638) 
  gradient.
* **NO busy elements beyond the thumbnail cascade and character.**
* **NO text on the thumbnail.** The title does all the textual work.

---

# 5. COMPOSITION & COLOR:
* **Character position:** Center, taking up ~40% of frame width.
* **Phone held up at upper-right area of frame** — prominent, screen 
  clearly visible.
* **Thumbnail cascade:** Behind and around the character, filling 
  the background.
* **Color palette:** Deep dark background vs. Purple/Cyan rim lights 
  vs. Phone glow vs. Thumbnail cascade glow.
* **NO TEXT on the thumbnail** (the timer on the phone screen is part 
  of the image, not overlaid text).

---

# NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
* NO flat lighting.
* NO text overlay (the timer on the phone screen is rendered as part 
  of the image).
* NO oversaturated red skin.
* NO "blown out" whites.
* NO cartoonish phone or thumbnails — keep it photorealistic and 
  cinematic.
* NO identical thumbnails in the cascade.

---

# GOAL:
A highly clickable, cinematic thumbnail. The man confidently holds up 
proof of speed (20-minute timer) while a cascade of professional 
thumbnails fans out behind him. The viewer instantly understands: 
"This person made ALL these thumbnails in just 20 minutes." The vibe 
combines speed, volume, and professional quality — irresistible for 
any content creator.
```
