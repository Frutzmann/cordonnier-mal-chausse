Anatomie du Prompt Structure

Chaque section a un role precis dans la generation

ROLE
You are a World-Class YouTube Thumbnail Designer & CTR Strategist...
Cadre le style et le niveau de qualite
TASK
Create a dramatic thumbnail showing...
Decrit ce que le modele doit creer
CHARACTER
Pose: Right hand holding up a smartphone...
Skin Texture: Hyper-realistic but "polished"...
Controle le rendu physique du personnage
LIGHTING
Key Light: Soft, bright light...
Dual Rim Lights: Purple (#9D4F9E) left, Cyan right...
Cree la profondeur 3D et le "pop"
HERO VISUAL
8-10 YouTube thumbnails fanned out like a deck of cards...
L'element cle qui raconte l'histoire
BACKGROUND
Deep dark background, near black with subtle dark navy gradient...
Fond propre = sujet lisible
COMPOSITION
Character centered, ~40% of frame. Phone at upper-right.
Color palette: Purple/Cyan vs dark...
Equilibre visuel et harmonie des couleurs
Sections fixes (pre-remplies)
NEGATIVE
NO flat lighting. NO text overlay. NO oversaturated red skin. NO cartoonish elements...
Empeche les erreurs classiques de l'IA
GOAL
A highly clickable, cinematic thumbnail. The viewer instantly understands...
Oriente toute la generation vers le resultat
